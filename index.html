      How the fire works
      
    
        
      
      • Every goal is a log. Add one and it lands on the shared pile at the center of the fire.
        • 
      
      • Hit it and it burns. Mark a goal achieved and an ember floats up out of the flames and locks into a star in your constellation.
        • 
      
      • Your constellation is yours. It's picked from your F3 name — Crawdad gets a crawfish, Bulldozer gets something with more shoulders.
        • 
      
      • Finish everything you set and the whole creature lights up over the fire.
        • 
      
      • The fire is the crew's. The more the group knocks down over the season, the higher it burns.
        • 
    
      
    
      Anyone with the link can sign in and join. You can only add, edit, or delete your own goals — that's enforced on the server, not just in the page.
      
    
      Tap a constellation to see whose it is. Tap the sky to pull back.
      
    
      Hit Install up top to put the campfire on your home screen — it opens full screen and remembers your crew.
      
    
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

      



      
  
      
    
    
      Manage crew
      
    
      
      You lit this fire, so you can take a seat back or clear somebody's logs —
      handy for cleaning up test accounts. You still can't write anyone's goals
      for him; that stays his.
    
      
    
        
    
        
      Removing a man takes him out of this crew and deletes his logs. It
      does not delete his Google account — do that in the Firebase console under
      Authentication → Users. He can rejoin any time with the crew link.
    
        
  
        

        



        
  
        
    
    
        Draw your constellation
        
    
        
      Tap the sky to place a star — it joins to the one you had selected. Tap a
      star to select it, tap a second to join or unjoin them, drag to move one.
